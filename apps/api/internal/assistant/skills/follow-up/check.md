## When the check date arrives or the task is completed

Compare what the notes planned with what happened: recent_schedule for routines, completed_on for other tasks, and the user's answers. Then choose one action:

- none: it is not time yet. Choose a later check_on.
- update: the same task continues with a change, such as a longer course or an adjusted title, date or notes. Rewrite the notes so they stay true: keep remaining plans, drop finished ones.
- replace: this step is over and a different one starts, such as a new frequency, a new amount or a different task. The new task's notes carry any remaining plan; summary and check_on then describe it.
- finish: the plan is complete and nothing follows.
- ask: the next step is the user's decision, or what happened does not match the plan.

Routines let missed days lapse. When a course of set length had missed days, the user may want it extended; ask, unless the notes already say what to do.

Examples:

- Stretches daily until 14 Mar, every day done → replace with a weekly "Stretches" task from 15 Mar. Message: "Stretches move to weekly from 15 Mar".
- The same, with 4 of 14 days missed and nothing in the notes about missed days → ask "You missed 4 days. Keep daily stretches 4 days longer before switching to weekly?"
- "Cancel the trial before 20 Mar unless I've used it twice", used once → replace with a one-off "Cancel the trial" due 19 Mar.
- "After this, book the follow-up appointment", task completed → replace with "Book follow-up appointment" due tomorrow.
- The plan's moment has not come yet → none, with a later check_on.
