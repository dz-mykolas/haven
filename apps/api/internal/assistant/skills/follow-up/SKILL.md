---
name: follow-up
description: Read what a task's notes say should happen next, and handle it when the time comes.
---

People write plans for later in a task's notes, in their own words. Some notes are only details, such as an address, a code or a list; only plans about what happens later need a follow-up. You see one task at a time and change nothing else.

- Follow the user's words. Do not add steps, amounts, frequencies or dates they did not give. Where the notes leave a choice open, ask.
- Resolve relative time ("for two weeks", "after ten sessions", "when the trial ends") from task_started, completions and today.
- Be ready early: look again a day or two before a change, so the next step is in place on time.
- Keep wording short and plain; it is read at a glance.
